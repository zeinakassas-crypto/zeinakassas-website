How much is the printed copy?

You pay the book price now. Printing and shipping depend on your address, so we calculate it and send you the exact cost on WhatsApp. You also receive the PDF once payment is confirmed.

Yes. Message us on Instagram @coachbigzee or email zeina.kassas@gmail.com and we'll send you an international payment option.
